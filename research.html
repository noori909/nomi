// Academic Direction & Exploration

Research & Inquiries

Undergraduate technical interests in high-frequency electromagnetics, antenna systems, space networks, and computational optimization.

These represent broad areas of active technical curiosity and future research exploration for graduate study—not claims of completed professional specialization.

Primary Research Areas

Domain 01

RF, Antennas & Microwave Systems

Planar and conformal antenna design, array architectures, high-frequency transmission lines, and active microwave front-ends.

Planar & Conformal Antennas Phased Arrays Beamforming & Beam Steering Reconfigurable Metasurfaces mmWave Technologies RF Front-Ends RFIC & High-Frequency Devices Power Amplifiers
Domain 02

Wireless, Radar & Space Communications

Next-generation mobile communication architectures, non-terrestrial networks, satellite transceivers, and radar sensing systems.

6G Communications Satellite Communications (SATCOM) Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN) Radar & Remote Sensing Long-Range Wireless Communications
Domain 03

Applied Electromagnetics & Intelligent Engineering

Wave scattering, radar cross section control, material absorption, and machine-learning-assisted parameter optimization for high-frequency antenna and microwave structures (distinct from mechanical MEMS or robotics).

Radar Cross Section (RCS) Reduction Stealth & Absorption (RAM) EM Scattering Modeling Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) Electromagnetic Shielding ML-Assisted EM Optimization Surrogate Modeling Computational Antenna Design

Adjacent Engineering Interests

High-Frequency Electronics Linear Control Systems Computational Modeling
