Thoughts and Surroundings

Changes in your physical surroundings alter the thoughts that arise within you.

Listening to conversation, criticism, praise, random speech, music, or background noise directly shifts your internal mood and the kinds of thoughts that naturally emerge.

I have noticed that changing environments produces thoughts that later feel strange or unfamiliar when revisited in a different setting.

What enters the mind from the surrounding environment heavily shapes what happens inside it.
