A Strange Fantasy

The smoke hangs low over the quieted earth. Lying wounded amidst the stillness of a fallen battlefield, the body grows cold while the mind remains piercingly clear.

In these final moments of endurance, thoughts turn not to fear, but to memory, identity, and the quiet acceptance of mortality—contemplating what it means to be forgotten and whether a life held quiet, unwritten worth.

"Death can have me when it has earned me."

The sky above holds no visual imagery, only the deep, silent space of acceptance.
