About Me

Electrical Engineering undergraduate deeply fascinated by electromagnetics.

Who I Am

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate student at Balochistan University of Information Technology, Engineering and Management Sciences (BUITEMS), Quetta, with an academic direction in Telecommunications.

Rather than confining myself prematurely to a single narrow engineering box, my curiosity naturally crosses disciplines. Electrical Engineering serves as the broader umbrella under which I explore electronics, communication systems, network architectures, embedded computing, signals, control, and machine learning. From this wide engineering foundation, a particularly deep fascination with Radio Frequency (RF) systems, antennas, and applied electromagnetics has emerged as one of my strongest directions of inquiry.

I see this website as both a professional engineering portfolio and an evolving personal archive: a place to document technical work, track academic progression, and preserve reflections.

What I Study

My academic progression spans mathematics, physical field theory, electronic design, computational systems, and communication networks. The completed coursework through six semesters forms five interconnected pillars:

1. Mathematical & Physical Foundations

Calculus & Analytical Geometry, Applied Physics, Differential Equations, Linear Algebra, Complex Variables & Transforms, and Probability Methods in Engineering.

2. Electrical & Electronic Systems

Linear Circuit Analysis, Workshop Practice, Electronic Devices & Circuits, Electrical Network Analysis, Digital Logic Design, Electrical Machines, and Electronic Circuit Design.

3. Signals, Electromagnetics & Control

Signals & Systems, Electromagnetic Field Theory, and Linear Control Systems.

4. Computing & Embedded Systems

Computing Fundamentals, Programming Fundamentals, Data Structures & Algorithms, Embedded Systems, and Machine Learning.

5. Communications & Networking

Computer Communication Networks and Communication Systems.

Computing as an Engineering Toolkit

I regularly write code, explore algorithms, and build software—from native Android applications and desktop systems to microcontroller firmware and machine learning models. However, computing is part of my Electrical Engineering toolkit, not a separate degree identity.

Being comfortable bridging physical electronics, networking protocols, hardware interfaces, and computational tools allows me to analyze complex systems with both physical hardware intuition and software fluency. That breadth across disciplines is the point.

What I Am Working Toward

As an undergraduate, I am focused on building a solid foundational core while developing real depth in high-frequency engineering. My current Final Year Project (FYP) involves designing and simulating a flexible MIMO antenna system for millimeter-wave IoT devices using full-wave solvers (CST Studio Suite and Ansys HFSS).

I enjoy understanding how systems work from first principles and look forward to refining this foundation through advanced research and graduate-level engineering.
