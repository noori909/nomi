About Me

Electrical Engineering undergraduate deeply fascinated by electromagnetics.

Background

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate student at Balochistan University of Information Technology, Engineering and Management Sciences (BUITEMS), Quetta, with an official academic direction in Telecommunications.

My studies span circuit analysis, electronic devices, signal processing, communication networks, control systems, and embedded computing. From this broad foundation, I have developed a strong emerging focus in Radio Frequency (RF) engineering, antennas, and applied electromagnetics.

Approach to Engineering

I approach engineering practically: understanding physical wave behavior, building circuits, programming microcontrollers, and solving field equations through numerical simulation.

Computing—whether writing C/C++ for an ESP32, writing MATLAB scripts for transfer functions, or training machine learning models—is an integral part of my engineering toolkit rather than a separate degree identity. Connecting hardware, network stacks, and computational tools allows me to approach problems with both physical intuition and software fluency.

Current Work

My Final Year Project (FYP) focuses on designing and simulating a flexible MIMO antenna system for millimeter-wave IoT devices using CST Studio Suite and Ansys HFSS. I aim to build on this work as I prepare for graduate-level research and high-frequency engineering.
