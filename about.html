About Me

Electrical Engineering undergraduate deeply fascinated by electromagnetics.

Who I Am

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate student at Balochistan University of Information Technology, Engineering and Management Sciences (BUITEMS), Quetta, with a focus on telecommunications, RF systems, antennas, and applied electromagnetics.

I view engineering as an active laboratory—a space to understand physical wave behavior, build real hardware, write code, and solve physical field problems rather than just memorizing formulas.

What I Study

My academic progression spans mathematics, physical field theory, electronic design, computational systems, and communication networks. The completed coursework through the 6th semester naturally builds across five core pillars:

1. Mathematical & Physical Foundations

Calculus & Analytical Geometry, Applied Physics, Differential Equations, Linear Algebra, Complex Variables & Transforms, and Probability Methods.

2. Electrical & Electronic Systems

Linear Circuit Analysis, Workshop Practice, Electronic Devices & Circuits, Electrical Network Analysis, Digital Logic Design, Electrical Machines, and Electronic Circuit Design.

3. Signals, Electromagnetics & Control

Signals & Systems, Electromagnetic Field Theory, and Linear Control Systems.

4. Computing & Embedded Systems

Computing Fundamentals, Programming Fundamentals, Data Structures & Algorithms, Embedded Systems, and Machine Learning.

5. Communications & Networking

Computer Communication Networks and Communication Systems.

Development & Exploration

Alongside coursework, I actively explore microcontrollers (working with ESP32 modules for network security), software development (Pharmacy POS desktop application, native Android applications, Python 2D games), and full-wave electromagnetic simulation in CST Studio Suite and Ansys HFSS.

Personal Outlook

"All men have limits... I ignore mine." — Knightfall

Outside engineering, I am interested in cinema (especially Sci-Fi, survival thrillers, and the works of Christopher Nolan and Denis Villeneuve), language learning, Batman and Star Wars lore, FPS multiplayer games, and writing personal reflections.
