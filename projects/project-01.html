A Flexible MIMO Antenna System for Next-Gen Millimeter Wave IoT Enabled Smart Devices

Design, physical conformability evaluation, and 3D full-wave electromagnetic simulation of a flexible multi-port MIMO antenna array operating at millimeter-wave frequencies.

1. Overview & Objective

Next-generation IoT smart devices and body-centric wearables demand high data throughput, low latency, and conformable physical form factors. This project focuses on designing a multi-element (MIMO) antenna printed on flexible dielectric substrates operating at millimeter-wave (mmWave) frequency bands.

2. Design Considerations

  • Substrate Conformability: Evaluating return loss ($S_{11}$) stability under physical bending conditions.
  • Mutual Coupling Reduction: Exploring decoupling mechanisms to ensure inter-port isolation ($S_{21} < -20\text{ dB}$).
  • Diversity Performance: Calculating Envelope Correlation Coefficient (ECC) and Diversity Gain (DG).

3. 3D Full-Wave EM Simulations

Using CST Studio Suite and Ansys HFSS to analyze far-field radiation patterns, gain characteristics, and surface current distributions across planar and flexed configurations.

4. Current Status

Active undergraduate final year research project focused on simulation, parameter optimization, and performance analysis.
